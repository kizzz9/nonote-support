FAQFrequently asked

1How do I play?

Tap or trace to fill cells, and double-tap to place an ×. The numbers on each row and column tell you how many filled cells run together. Practise any time from Settings → How to solve.

2Removing the ads between puzzles

Buy Settings → Remove ads once and no more video ads or bottom banners appear, and hints become unlimited. On another iPhone with the same Apple ID, use Restore purchases.

3My purchase doesn't show up

Reopen the app. If it still does not appear, tap Settings → Restore purchases. Refunds are requested from Apple; once refunded, what the purchase unlocked is no longer available.

4Does it cost anything to play?

No. The first cat, every song and the lessons are free. Hints are free three times a day, and a short video adds more. Only extras, such as letting one more cat out at a time or removing ads, can be bought.

5Moving to a new iPhone

Sign in to iCloud with the same Apple ID on the new iPhone and your collected tunes come back automatically.

6Joining or leaving the rankings

Turn on Settings → Game Center rankings to join. Turn it off and nothing more is sent.

7Deleting my data

Deleting the app removes everything stored on the device. Records left in iCloud can be managed in iOS Settings → Apple ID → iCloud. See the privacy policy.
